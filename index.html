About Me

I'm Chenxu Bai (白晨旭), an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science at School of Electronics Engineering and Computer Science, Peking University.

My background lies in the intersection of computer vision and computational photography. More recently, I have been exploring multimodal foundation models.

I am currently a research intern at Xiaomi MiMo team, where I am working on multimodal models, especially unified models for multimodal understanding and generation. I am also a research intern at PKU-Yuan-Lab.
